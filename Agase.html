Agase
(Sesbania grandiflora)

About

Agase (Sesbania grandiflora) is a versatile, fast-growing,nitrogen-fixing
tree widely valued for its role in agroforestry, soil improvement,
and sustainable agriculture.Native to South and Southeast Asia, it thrives in
tropical and subtropical regions and has been cultivated globally for its
ecological and economic benefits.It is commonly grown along field bunds,
rice paddies,and around farmsteads,where it enhances soil fertility and
prevents erosion.Due to its open canopy structure, it allows ample sunlight
penetration,making it an ideal intercrop companion for black pepper,coffee,
tea,and betel vine.

Beyond its ecological benefits,Agati is an important food source.Its leaves,
flowers, and young pods are highly nutritious and commonly consumed in
traditional cuisines.The tree is also a valuable fodder crop for livestock,
providing high-protein green forage.Additionally,Agati is widely used in
traditional medicine for its antibacterial,anti-inflammatory,and wound-
healing properties.It serves multiple purposes,including green manure,
erosion control,pulpwood,and fiber production,making it a key species in
regenerative farming and sustainable land management.
