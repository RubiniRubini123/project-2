Our Story

Agrilife Sciences is a boutique firm, founded by visionary technocrat
and agriculture postgraduate, Sanjeev Asagodu. Our firm was
founded in 2005 dedicated to preserving and enhancing soil fertility.

Inspired by the Vedas.

Guided by experience.

Veda means knowledge—to know and understand the creator of life.
As human beings, we have no right to live at the cost of
nature our primary duty is to protect and sustain it. Soil is a great
platform on which all creatures live. The seeds we grow in it help
to sustain all microbes, fix atmospheric nitrogen in the soil, and
promote increased food production. The essence of our Vedas
signifies the worship of nature.

Beginning his career with multinational companies that produced
and marketed pesticides, he witnessed firsthand the
environmental impact and decided to change direction. Drawing
inspiration from the Vedas, he transitioned to embracing organic
green manure and natural products, ultimately founding Agrilife Sciences in 2005 to advocate for sustainable agricultural practices.

We value Sanjeev's thought process, which is shaped by his extensive experience, knowledge and direct interactions with farmers for over 40 years. His journey from conventional pesticide use to advocating for organic solutions reflects our commitment to nurturing the soil and preserving nature for future generations.
