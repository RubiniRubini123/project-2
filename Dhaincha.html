Dhaincha
(Sesbania aculeata)

About

Dhaincha (Sesbania aculeata) is a robust,fast-growing,nitrogen-
fixing legume widely cultivated as a green manure, cover crop,and
fodder species.It is highly valued for its ability to improve soil fertility,
making it an essential crop for sustainable agriculture.Dhaincha
is particularly suited for saline, alkaline, and waterlogged soils wher
other crops struggle to grow. It is commonly used in crop rotations to
restore soil health and enhance productivity.

Apart from its soil-improving properties, Dhaincha provides high-
protein fodder for livestock, making it an excellent dual-purpose crop.
It also plays a significant role in erosion control due to its deep root
system, which stabilizes soil and prevents degradation.In addition,its
stems are used for fuelwood, fiber production,and paper
manufacturing. This versatile crop is an integral part of regenerative
farming systems, particularly in tropical and subtropical climates.

Applications and advantages

Soil Fertility & Nitrogen Fixation

Enriches soil with organic
matter and fixes nitrogen(up
to 100-120 kg/ha),reducing
fertilizer dependency.

Agroforestry & Cover
Crop

Acts as a protective cover crop
in intercropping systems,
suppressing weeds and
conserving soil moisture.

Erosion Control

Deep root system binds
soil, preventing erosion and
enhancing soil structure.

Fodder & Forage

Provides nutrient-rich green
biomass for livestock, improving
animal health and productivity

Timber & Fiber

Stems are used for making fuelwood,
ropes,paper, and
mats,adding economic value.

Salinity & Waterlogging
Tolerance

Thrives in saline,alkaline,and
waterlogged soils, making
it ideal for land reclamation.

Drought Resistance

Hardy crop that thrives in arid
and semi-arid conditions with
minimal water requirements.
